The lanterns of the world have gone out. Guide Pip through three dark realms, gather every ember and open the portal home.

Play now

The three realms

1. Whispering Woods

Slimes guard the paths and the pits are deep. A gentle start.

2. Crystal Caverns

Only your lantern lights the way. Ride the moving platform across the chasm.

3. Molten Core

The floor is lava and fire spirits drift in the heat. Do not touch them.

How to play

  • Move with the left and right arrow keys. Jump with the up arrow. Press P to pause.
  • Collect every ember to open the portal to the next realm.
  • Jump on slimes and bats to defeat them. Fire spirits cannot be defeated.
  • Spikes, lava, pits and enemies each cost a life. You start with three lives and win one more for every realm you finish.
  • Log in before you play so your best score appears on the rankings.
